Frequently asked questions

Before you place an order

Why are prices not displayed?

Gadget prices and stock can change. WhatsApp lets the store confirm the current price for the exact model, storage, colour and condition you want.

Can I visit the physical shop?

Yes. Megabros Enterprise is at 11 Pepple Street, Computer Village, Ikeja, Lagos. Message or call before visiting if you want to confirm a specific model.

Do you sell more than iPhones?

Yes. You can request Android phones, laptops, tablets, iPads and accessories through the gadget finder or WhatsApp. The current online photo catalog focuses on iPhones.

Can I ask for a particular storage or colour?

Yes. Add the model, storage, colour and preferred condition to your WhatsApp message. The store will confirm what is currently available.

Is delivery available?

Delivery options can be discussed based on your location and the item. Confirm the delivery method, timing and cost before payment.
